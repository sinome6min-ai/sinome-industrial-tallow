06 / FAQ

Before you request a quote.

Can SINOME send a fixed industrial tallow specification immediately?

No universal specification is published. Send your acceptance limits first; we then assess a current Mongolian lot against them.

Where does the material come from?

The current source country is Mongolia. The exact supplier and facility are disclosed during qualification under appropriate commercial conditions.

Can the product be imported into Malaysia?

Potentially, but shipment must wait until the Malaysian importer confirms the current DVS and MAQIS requirements for the exact product, Mongolian facility and certificate wording.

How is the loading format selected?

The format is confirmed only after product behaviour, service-provider requirements, route conditions and the receiver’s discharge capability have been reviewed.

Do you publish a price?

No. Industrial tallow pricing depends on specification, lot, quantity, heating and packaging plan, route, Incoterm, currency, payment terms and offer validity.

What should a qualified buyer provide?

Company identity, intended use, destination port, expected volume, specification limits, document requirements, Incoterm and target delivery window.
